Neuroscience Lessons

Interactive simulations of how neurons work. Start with lesson 1; each one builds on the last.

  1. 01 Membrane Potential Watch ions cross a membrane one by one and see where the resting voltage comes from: charge and capacitance, the Nernst potential, and the GHK equation.
  2. 02 RC Circuit Treat the membrane as a resistor and capacitor: how voltage charges up over time, and what sets the time constant.
